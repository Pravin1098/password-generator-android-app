# SecurePass – Password Generator Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026  
**Developer:** codeshadow  
**Privacy contact:** codeshadow98@gmail.com

SecurePass – Password Generator ("SecurePass," "the app," "we," or "our") is an offline-first utility for generating passwords, passphrases, and PINs. This policy describes what the app stores and how that information is handled.

## Summary

- Passwords, passphrases, and PINs are generated on your device.
- SecurePass does not require an account and does not send generated secrets to our servers. The app has no backend service.
- Saved history and favorites stay in local device storage. Saving history is controlled by the in-app setting; favoriting a value saves it locally.
- Copying a value places it on your device clipboard, which is managed by your operating system and may be accessible to other apps or synced according to your device settings.

## Information SecurePass handles

### Generated values

Generation takes place locally on your device. A generated value remains in the app's current memory while displayed. If password history is enabled, generated values are saved in the app's local secure storage. Turning history off prevents new values from being added to history; it does not itself erase entries already saved. You can remove entries individually or clear history in the app.

If you mark a value as a favorite, SecurePass saves it in local secure storage until you delete it or clear the app's local data. Favorites are separate from the history setting.

### Settings

Theme, history preference, default length, haptic preference, and password-generation options are stored locally using the platform's application storage. These settings are not sent to us.

### Clipboard

When you choose Copy, SecurePass writes the selected generated or saved value to the device clipboard. Clipboard retention, access by other apps, and clipboard synchronization are controlled by your operating system and device settings. SecurePass does not clear the clipboard automatically. You can replace or clear clipboard contents using your device's controls.

## How information is used and shared

Local settings and saved values are used only to provide the app's features on your device. SecurePass does not operate an account system, advertising, analytics, or a backend, and does not sell, rent, or transmit generated values or settings to us or other parties.

The app's app-store distribution and device operating system are provided by third parties. Those providers may process information under their own policies when you download or use their platform services. Clipboard behavior is also controlled by the operating system.

## Storage, security, and retention

Settings use local application storage. History and favorites use the device secure-storage facility provided through Expo SecureStore, backed by platform security mechanisms. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or the app may be able to view saved values.

Saved entries remain on the device until you delete them, clear the relevant data in the app, or the operating system removes app data. Device backup, restore, and uninstall behavior may vary by platform and operating-system version. To remove saved app data from within SecurePass, use **Settings → Clear all local data**; history can also be cleared from its screen. Clearing app data does not necessarily clear the system clipboard or copies stored elsewhere by the user or operating system.

## Children's privacy

SecurePass does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. The app has no account creation or online service. If you believe information was sent to us in error, contact us at the address above; do not include passwords or other secrets in your message.
